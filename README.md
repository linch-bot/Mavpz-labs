**Domain:** A standard online retail platform where customers can browse products, add them to a shopping cart, and place orders. 

**Key Business Entities:**
- **Customer:** A registered user who can browse and purchase products.
- **Product:** An item available for sale in the store.
- **Category:** A grouping of products for easier navigation (e.g., "Electronics", "Clothing").
- **Order:** A record of a completed purchase made by a Customer.
